# AI Assistant Use

## What did I ask an AI assistant to do?

- I asked the AI assistant to alter a few of my photos in an attempt to fool my model.
- I also asked for help formatting sections of my README, especially creating tables.

## What did the AI assistant suggest that I did not accept?

- It suggested completely altering some photos so that they no longer included a pen or pencil.
- That guaranteed that the photos would not work, but it did not match my goal.
- I wanted to confuse the model, not give it an easy way to reject the photo.

## What did I not understand when I pressed "Train" in Teachable Machine?

- I had trouble figuring out how to use the different options.
- I did not understand how the webcam feature could capture 50 different pictures of a pen within five seconds.
- It might work for a single pen in one environment, but this project required different locations and more time.
- Overall, I did not understand the speed and quick pace of the feature.
